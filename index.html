MySQL 8Power BIPython

Customer Segmentation & Retention Analysis

Who are the valuable customers, who is being lost, and do new customers come back? Answered on 5,852 customers of a UK gift wholesaler.

Power BI dashboard
68.4%of revenue comes from the 1,390 Champions.
86%of revenue comes from repeat orders, only 14% from first orders.
59.1%of 4,305 new customers did not buy again within 90 days.
711At Risk customers spent £2,157 on average and have been silent for about a year.
DataUK online gift wholesaler, Dec 2009 to Dec 2011. 1.07 million line items cleaned into 43,876 invoices from 5,852 customers.
MethodPython script removes duplicates, returns and non-product lines. MySQL views score recency, frequency and spend into 6 segments, plus cohort retention. Power BI dashboard with 3 pages.
Next stepTest a win-back offer on the At Risk group with a control group, and test a follow-up within 30 days of a first order.
LimitsRule-based segments, one business, two years of data, no control group. It shows patterns, not causes.

E-commerce Sales & Promotion Analysis

Which products and promotions drive sales, and are the discounts worth what they cost? Answered on 3,510 orders over four years.

Power BI dashboard
73.7%of net sales come from 5 Electronics products.
73.4%of all discount money also went to Electronics, which sells well without help.
2.03units per order with a promotion and without one. Discounts did not make baskets bigger.

DataPublic e-commerce sample dataset: 3,510 orders, 50 customers, 30 products, 16 cities, 2020 to 2023, in rupees.
Method4-table MySQL star schema with 0 arithmetic or orphan-key errors on validation, 14 documented SQL queries using CTEs, window functions and joins, and a 3-page Power BI dashboard with slicers.
Next stepCap discounts near 20% and A/B test promotions before running them at scale.
LimitsSmall sample with 50 customers, and no control group, so the promotion comparison is not proof.
